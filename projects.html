Case Study · Brand Strategy
Tata Nano vs MG Comet — Why Positioning Matters More Than Price
Independent Research · Dec 2025
A strategic case study comparing two compact cars in the Indian market — arguing that Nano failed not because of the product, but because "world's cheapest car" signalled compromise in an aspirational market. MG Comet succeeded by selling identity (urban, youthful, conscious) rather than price. A sharp lesson in how positioning shapes perception — and perception shapes demand.
